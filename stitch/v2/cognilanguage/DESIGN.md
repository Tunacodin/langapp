## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an editorial, high-clarity environment inspired by modern hospitality and travel aesthetics, tailored specifically for structured linguistic mastery. Rejecting juvenile gamification, fluorescent streak tickers, and loud illustrations, the brand embodies mature focus, deliberate practice, and cognitive lightness. It serves ambitious self-directed learners, polyglots, and professionals seeking fluency through evidence-based acquisition models: FSRS (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler), Shadowing audio loops, i+1 Comprehensible Input scaffolding, and phraseological Chunking.

The aesthetic leans strictly into pristine architectural minimalism:
- Pure white planar surfaces grounded by subtle, crisp borders.
- Typographic hierarchy where scale, weight, and tracking command visual weight over decorative graphics.
- High-contrast visual breathing room designed to mitigate cognitive fatigue during dense recall intervals.
- Tactile, deliberate accents reserved strictly for functional interaction and communicative state changes.

## Colors

The palette establishes pristine visual hygiene through stark neutral surfaces, sharp boundaries, and concentrated bursts of communicative color.

### Functional Palette Application
- **Primary Accent (`#FF385C` - Babu Red):** Dedicated exclusively to high-conviction primary conversion points, audio capture/shadowing recording triggers, and core forward navigation. It is never used for static display or background washes.
- **Secondary Accent (`#008489` - Deep Teal):** Dedicated to structural linguistic mechanics: grammar token indicators, syntactic role tags, contextual pattern markers, and chunking boundaries.
- **Neutrals:**
  - **Surface Default (`#FFFFFF`):** Base canvas, card face, and primary viewport foundation.
  - **Surface Subtle (`#F7F7F7`):** Recessed target inputs, inactive audio scrub tracks, and segmented control wells.
  - **Border Crisp (`#EAEAEA`):** Standard 1px boundary across all cards, containers, input states, and persistent navigation bars.
  - **Text Dominant (`#222222`):** Primary display headings, target-language phrases, and core interactive labels.
  - **Text Muted (`#717171`):** Native language translations, phonetics, metadata timestamps, and card count indicators.
- **Pedagogical & FSRS Status Colors:**
  - **Again / Failure (`#EF4444`):** Strict retention lapses, speech mispronunciations, and resets.
  - **Hard (`#F59E0B`):** Delayed cognitive recall, challenging syntax markers.
  - **Good / Success (`#10B981`):** Target mastery thresholds, correct shadowing alignment, successful retention intervals.
  - **Easy (`#008489`):** Fluid, automatic comprehension intervals.

## Typography

Typographic discipline provides the structural backbone of this design system. Inter is applied system-wide to achieve clinical optical clarity, precise diacritic placement across diverse global character sets, and exact numeric layout during interval pacing.

- **Scale & Rhythm:** Typography replaces heavy decorative dividers. Transitions between the prominent display headers (`#222222`) and secondary native translations (`#717171`) create structural hierarchy without clutter.
- **Linguistic Chunking (`target-chunk-lg`):** Calibrated with heightened line-height (1.4–1.5x) to accommodate bidirectional syntax tagging, ruby text (furigana/pinyin), and sub-word chunk delimiters without vertical collisions.
- **Tracking Protocols:** Tightened negative tracking on all headings (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) creates a refined editorial silhouette. Positive letter-spacing (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) is applied strictly to micro labels, metadata, and grammar badges to maintain legibility at sub-12px scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid with a fluid 12-column foundation across larger screens and a fluid 4-column structure on mobile.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` outer canvas margin, `1rem` column gutters. Core interactive controls, audio recording triggers, and FSRS response docks anchor permanently to the viewport base.
  - **Tablet (768px–1023px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` outer margin, `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers constrain to a maximum width of 720px for optimal reading length.
  - **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12-column layout, `3rem` outer margin, `2rem` gutters. Spaced-repetition viewports and shadowing interfaces center within a rigid 960px maximum column shell, preventing wide, illegible eye travel during high-speed drills.
- **Spacing Principle:** Whitespace is treated as active structural reinforcement. Cards and pedagogical blocks leverage `space-lg` (1.5rem) internal breathing room, while phrase tokens within comprehension chunks use `space-xs` (0.25rem) to preserve word association.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, stacked ambient blurs, and skeuomorphic shading. Depth is engineered strictly through micro-borders and plane separation:

- **Flat Planar Differentiation:** All baseline containers (lesson modules, audio cards, syntax tables) rest on pure `#FFFFFF` bounded by a continuous 1px solid `#EAEAEA` stroke.
- **Zero Shadow Elevation:** Raised surfaces do not generate dark diffusions. Instead, interactive lift or floating sheets leverage an imperceptible ultra-low tint outline (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`) with zero blur, maintaining pristine clarity.
- **Overlay & Layering:** Modals and bottom sheets rest over an ambient dimmed wash (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`). The sheet itself uses an opaque `#FFFFFF` background with crisp perimeter isolation.
- **Active Selection Depth:** In lieu of shadows, selected states (such as active multiple-choice chunks or highlighted target words) introduce a 2px inward focus stroke using `#222222` or `#008489`, or transition their background to `#F7F7F7`.

## Shapes

Corner radii adhere to strict categorical roles:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Fixed `12px` border radius. Applies to flashcard review canvases, audio transcription blocks, reading view panels, and comprehension modules.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Inputs:** Fixed `8px` border radius. Applied to standard actions, text fields, audio scrub segments, and utility triggers.
- **Pills, Tags, and Syntax Badges:** Fixed `20px` (fully rounded pill geometry). Reserved for grammar roles, i+1 comprehension state indicators, audio speed selectors (e.g., 0.75x, 1.0x), and chunk boundaries.
- **Bottom Sheets & Modal Shelves:** Distinctive asymmetric rounding featuring `24px` on the top-left and top-right corners, with `0px` on the bottom edges to seal to the screen frame.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** `#FF385C` solid fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `8px` border radius, `0px` border. Minimum tap height `48px`. Active tap state dims brightness to 92% without vertical displacement.
- **Secondary Button:** `#FFFFFF` fill, `#222222` text, 1px `#222222` border, `8px` border radius.
- **Tertiary / Subdued:** `#FFFFFF` fill, `#717171` text, 1px `#EAEAEA` border. Transitions to `#222222` border on hover.
- **FSRS Decision Cluster:** A contextual four-button split matrix deployed during recall:
  - *Again:* `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#EF4444` border, `#EF4444` text.
  - *Hard:* `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#F59E0B` border, `#F59E0B` text.
  - *Good:* `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#10B981` border, `#10B981` text.
  - *Easy:* `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#008489` border, `#008489` text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Syntax / Chunking Chip:** Pill-shaped (`20px` radius), 1px solid border with subtle tinted fills:
  - Grammatical Marker: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#008489` border, `#008489` text.
  - i+1 Target Vocabulary: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#FF385C` border, `#FF385C` text.
  - Neutral Metadata: `#F7F7F7` fill, 1px `#EAEAEA` border, `#717171` text.

### Audio Shadowing Module
- Consists of a `#FFFFFF` card bounded by 1px `#EAEAEA` containing a dual-track waveform visualizer. Native track renders in `#717171`; user shadowing track overlaps in real-time in `#FF385C`.
- Includes a centered `56px` circular record button (`#FF385C` fill, `#FFFFFF` microphone icon) flanked by speed control pills (`0.8x`, `1.0x`) bounded by 1px `#EAEAEA`.

### Cards & Comprehension Blocks
- Enclosed in 1px `#EAEAEA` borders over pure `#FFFFFF`.
- Internal structure: `1.5rem` padding. Target language chunk sits prominently at `headline-md` or `target-chunk-lg` in `#222222`. The i+1 novel word is marked with a 1px solid `#008489` bottom border underline. The translation remains occluded or set at `body-md` in `#717171`.

### Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Text Entry Fields:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#EAEAEA` border, `8px` radius, `body-lg` typography. Focus shifts border to 1.5px solid `#222222`. No drop shadow glows.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist geometry. Checkbox uses `4px` radius; radio uses full circle. Active state uses `#222222` fill with `#FFFFFF` glyph, preserving the high-contrast monochrome baseline.

### Bottom Sheets
- Anchored with `24px` top corner radius, `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px `#EAEAEA` top border. Features an internal top drag handle (36px wide, 4px height, `#EAEAEA` fill, fully rounded). Used for detailed grammar breakdowns, inflection tables, and context notes.
